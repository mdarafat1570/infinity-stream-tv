Using this website

This website provides information about Infinity Stream TV, plan options, content categories, device setup, and ways to contact the team. An enquiry is a request for information; it does not itself create a subscription, process a payment, or issue account credentials.

Confirm your subscription details

Before purchasing, confirm the current price, billing period, available content, supported devices, number of simultaneous connections, activation process, and applicable cancellation or refund terms directly with support.

Availability and compatibility

Content and device options can vary by plan, location, device model, operating system, and player. Category images illustrate the type of content being discussed. Ask for confirmation of any specific channel, title, app, or device that matters to you.

Trials and reseller arrangements

Trial availability, duration, and eligibility must be confirmed with support. Reseller enquiries are assessed separately, and any account access or commercial terms are provided only after the relevant arrangement is confirmed.

Account and setup information

Keep any account details provided to you private. Follow the usage limits and setup instructions confirmed for your plan. Do not send account passwords or other sensitive credentials through the public enquiry form.

Questions before purchase

If a detail is unclear, ask for written confirmation before paying. The subscription information confirmed during your enquiry should explain the service and terms you are agreeing to.

Check the terms before you pay

The website does not process subscription payments. Contact support before purchasing to confirm whether a refund or cancellation is available for your selected plan, along with any eligibility conditions, deadlines, and payment arrangements.

Request a cancellation or refund

Contact the team on WhatsApp with your plan name, purchase or activation date, and a brief explanation of your request. Include an order or transaction reference if you have one. Do not send full payment-card details, passwords, or private account credentials.

If you are having a setup issue

Share your device model, player, and a description of the problem so support can review the setup. You may also request clarification of the cancellation or refund terms that were confirmed when you purchased.

What happens next
